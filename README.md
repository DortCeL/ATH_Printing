AutoCAD Training Home (ATH) is one of the most prominent and trusted printing and CAD training institutions in Bangladesh. Established in 1994 as a private organization, ATH initially began its journey as a professional AutoCAD training institute at Farmgate, Dhaka.
Recognizing the growing demand for large-format technical printing in the architectural and engineering sectors, ATH expanded its services in 1996 by introducing Tracing and Ammonia (Blueprint & Offset Printing) using high-capacity plotters. Since then, ATH has become a reliable printing partner for numerous architectural firms, engineering consultancies, developers, and government organizations across Bangladesh.
With decades of experience, advanced machinery, skilled manpower, and a strong reputation for quality and reliability, ATH continues to serve as a one-stop solution for CAD training and professional printing services.
